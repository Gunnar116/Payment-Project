Minimums & Requirements

Known fees, floors, and terms by vendor

Stripe Connect Near-Term
  • No monthly minimum or processing volume floor
  • $2/mo per active connected account; 0.25% + $0.25 per payout to sub-merchants
  • Month-to-month; no ETF; T+2 card settlement; platforms can delay payouts up to 31 days
Adyen for Platforms Near / Long-Term
  • ~$1,100/mo minimum invoice floor (regardless of volume); reserves negotiated per contract
  • ~$500K+/mo processing volume to onboard (reported, not publicly stated)
  • 60-day cancellation notice; no ETF; T+2 settlement; strict 0.5% chargeback threshold
Braintree (PayPal) Validate First
  • No monthly fee or volume minimum; cards 2.89% + $0.29 per transaction
  • Marketplace is US-only, requires sales approval, and is incompatible with PayPal wallet and most recurring billing
  • Rolling reserves 5-10%, held 45-90 days; T+2 ACH; up to 21-day hold for new accounts
J.P. Morgan Payments Long-Term
  • All fees, minimums, and contract terms not publicly disclosed
  • Enterprise-only; requires direct sales and underwriting engagement; API currently in beta
  • Same-day settlement available via WePay/Chase infrastructure
Fiserv (Carat) Long-Term
  • Standard: $197/mo inactivity fee; $95-120/yr annual fee; ~$60/mo PCI fee
  • Carat/PayFac-as-a-Service fees not publicly disclosed; enterprise volume implied
  • Standard contracts: 3-year; ETF = 80% of avg monthly fees x remaining months
Global Payments / Worldpay Long-Term
  • Standard: $35/mo processing minimum; $50-100/mo account fee; $20-79/mo PCI fee
  • Platform (Payrix Pro) fees not disclosed; AmEx direct conversion at $1M/yr or $100K in any 3 consecutive months
  • Standard: 3-year contract; ETF $295-495/location; T+1 cards; same-day available with underwriting approval

Stripe Connect is the only provider with fully public pricing. All platform and PayFac-as-a-Service terms for Adyen, J.P. Morgan, Fiserv, and Global Payments/Worldpay require direct sales engagement. Validate all figures before committing.

KidSync · Final Takeaway
12 / 12

Conclusions

PayFac Analysis

Strongest near-term fit

Stripe + Stripe Connect: best developer ecosystem, marketplace payouts, 1099s, reserves. Validates PayFac model without full compliance burden.

Strong enterprise candidate

Adyen for Platforms: strongest cross-stage coverage for a company building toward PayFac. Evaluate in parallel if volume and enterprise readiness justify.

Long-term infrastructure candidates

J.P. Morgan Payments, Fiserv, Global Payments/Worldpay are relevant as KidSync proves volume and builds toward direct processor relationships. Not day-one decisions.

Suggested Next Steps

→Validate Stripe Connect terms for marketplace payments, recurring billing, and sub-merchant onboarding before committing.
→Request an Adyen for Platforms proposal for comparison. Confirm volume minimums and pricing tier.
→Clarify KidSync's merchant-of-record model: does KidSync take payment liability, or do individual providers?
→Validate Braintree marketplace and recurring billing compatibility if PayPal/Venmo wallet is a priority.
!Defer J.P. Morgan Payments, Fiserv, and Global Payments/Worldpay conversations until processing volume justifies the enterprise relationship.
!All long-term vendor terms should be validated directly. Do not treat this analysis as final pricing or contract terms.

"Start with Stripe Connect to prove the model. Run a parallel Adyen evaluation for enterprise scale. Treat J.P. Morgan Payments and Fiserv as the long-term infrastructure roadmap, not day-one decisions."
